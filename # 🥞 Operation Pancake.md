# 🥞 Operation Pancake

Operation Pancake is a research and decision-support platform for EA Sports College Football Ultimate Team.

The project is designed to turn validated player-card, progression, archetype, market, and gameplay research into reproducible roster and value decisions.

## Core Goals

- Maintain a canonical database of validated player cards.
- Reverse-engineer position and archetype OVR formulas.
- Preserve progression and upgrade research.
- Analyze player value and market opportunities.
- Identify Moneyball roster opportunities.
- Produce explainable GM recommendations.
- Preserve source provenance and research confidence.

## Engineering Principles

- Never guess missing or unreadable data.
- Preserve source provenance.
- Keep research evidence separate from production player records.
- Validate data before it enters the runtime system.
- Make calculations reproducible.
- Make recommendations explainable.
- Protect established research when new features are added.

## Status

Operation Pancake is currently under active development.
