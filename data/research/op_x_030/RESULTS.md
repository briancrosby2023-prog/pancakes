## Scope and controls

The complete canonical population was routed through the unchanged production models.
Attribute value means contribution to the validated Pancake ranking score. It is not an
independent gameplay claim, EA OVR coefficient, roster value, or market value.

contributions, score shares, peer percentiles, scarcity, local density, and exact marginal
score effects for +1/+2/+3/+5. Contributions reconcile to the production score. Profile or
coverage changes are explicitly flagged because normalized contribution can shift even
when a raw rating falls or is missing.
