# Scientific Safety

Required invariants:

- Context cannot mutate frozen score, rank, or percentile.
- Unknown context cannot create bonus or penalty.
- Recommendation cannot become observed usage.
- Sale cannot become football rejection.
- Canonical position cannot be overwritten by deployment.
- Theoretical build cannot become observed build.
- Ability availability cannot imply equipped.
- Equipped cannot imply competitive value.
- Contextual residuals do not automatically prove model error.
- Market evidence semantics remain unchanged.
