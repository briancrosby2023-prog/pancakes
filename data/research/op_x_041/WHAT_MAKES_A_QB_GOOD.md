# WHAT MAKES A QB GOOD IN CUT27?

## Supported facts

EA confirms that ability slots can be upgraded and Ability EVOs can add or change abilities. CFB.FAN cautions that an ability appearing in a bucket does not prove a card can equip it.

## Competitive preferences

One high-level author values passing release, Dot!, mobility, throw-on-run, and affordability alongside accuracy and throw power. The author preferred Kurt Benkert's release and criticized Robert Griffin III's release despite valuing Griffin's mobility.

## Supported thresholds

None have independent current corroboration.

## Source-specific thresholds

One current source explicitly uses **85+ THP** and **85+ MAC**. These are anecdotal source-specific thresholds, not Pancake-wide meta rules.

## Scheme-dependent requirements

Mobility and throw-on-run matter more for scramble/improvisation roles; no numerical SPD threshold was recovered.

## Contested claims

None established from the accepted sample.

## Unknown

SAC, DAC, ACC, COD, height, exact release-animation names, ability costs, pressure behavior, archetype rules, and option-specific numerical thresholds remain unknown.
