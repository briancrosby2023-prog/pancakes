# NMS QB Shopping Guide

## What do I actually need to pay for?

No numerical QB threshold is supported by independent competitive evidence yet. Do not pay a blanket premium merely to cross 85 THP or 85 MAC. The originating source's own budget guide treats 81 THP and 82–83 MAC QBs as viable budget options.

Screen for your scheme: arm strength and accuracy for intended throws, mobility for option/scramble use, and verified ability access/AP when available. Release animation remains an unmodeled unknown. All named budget cards require a current price check; this is not a BUY list.
