# OP-X-051 Product Surface

Implemented engine surfaces support product wiring for:

- `context CARD` — existing OP-X-050 command.
- `role-board POSITION ROLE` — candidate board, blocked when evidence/profile unsupported.
- `role-alternatives CARD ROLE` — binding-trait-preserving alternatives, market-independent.
- `roster-roles` — conservative roster role map; unsupported deployment returns DEPLOYMENT REQUIRED.
- `zero-coin-upgrades` — must remain empty/UNKNOWN unless deployment/ability evidence supports a no-purchase change.
- `target-challenge CARD ROLE` — full-population role alternatives can challenge complete-profile targets with specialist solutions.

GM answer contract: what the card is; candidate jobs; verified/unknown context; largest supported role-specific weakness; whether an evidence-backed zero-coin change exists; which football profile solves the job; what evidence is missing.
